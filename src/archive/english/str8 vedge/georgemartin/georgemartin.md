<p>• Staying silent on this ensures that the endless misinformation about veganism continues. Not only that, as we're seeing here, it is defended. There's a reason almost everyone in the world thinks that it's a diet. There's a reason so many people call it "a reduction of suffering". There's a reason why we have "vegan but cheese" trends. Because it's framed as those things: as a boycott of certain "products", as a calculative harm reduction movement, and so on. Misinformation has never been helpful for any justice movement, and obviously, it's detrimental here as well.</p>
