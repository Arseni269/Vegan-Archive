<p>✍️To actually argue against veganism is to defend the false notion that other species of animals should be perpetually commodified and exploited and we shouldn't change that.</p>
<p>📍Veganism isn't a set of practices but a principle, from which practices logically follow. It's a principle that humans should live without exploiting other animals.</p>
<p>🐄 It recognizes them as individuals deserving of a moral consideration, not objects to exploit. It's about unlearning the devaluing, objectifying conditioning which views them as disposables and slaves for the human animal.</p>
<p>Be vegan.</p>
