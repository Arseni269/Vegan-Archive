<p>As long as we see animals as products, animals will never be free from us(e). This is the objectification bias against animals; if we resolve this, it will lead to justice for animals, as it corrects the relationship between animals and humans. Be it a feather lying on the ground, an egg in the park, or simply animals in the wild, it doesn't give you the right to use them for your own benefit. Animals are not here to serve you.</p>
<p>Veganism is the principle that humanity is to live without exploiting animals.</p>
