<p>Veganism = End Animal Use
Veganism the principle that humanity should live without exploiting (using) animals. It is about rejecting the exploitative mindset that animals are commodities for human use.
In this illustration I wanted to highlight a specific tradition that children participates in, Easter. Children are encouraged to hunt for (in most cases, dairy) chocolate eggs, or decorate chicken eggs. It doesn't matter whether the eggs you want to decorate were from factory farming or backyard hens, the point is that you are still viewing the animal as something for you to use, in this case, as entertainment. Understand that animals do NOT belong to you. As long as human hold this exploitative mindset, animals will never be free from us.</p>
