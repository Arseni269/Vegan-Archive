<p>A vegan sees a horse and sees the individual; we do not see them as a vehicle, we do not see them as something for human consumption, we do not see them as entertainment. Veganism is about rejecting the exploitative belief that animals are here to serve us.</p>
<p>Veganism is the principle that humanity is to live without exploiting animals.</p>
