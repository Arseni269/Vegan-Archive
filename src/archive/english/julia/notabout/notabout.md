<p>Veganism is not a matter of choice, benefit, or social approval. It is not about the environment, health, or trends. Veganism is an ethical principle that humans should live without exploiting animals. It is not about improving the conditions of their exploitation, but about the complete abolition of their commodity status. About end animal use.</p>

<p>Veganism is not a diet or a lifestyle - it is basic justice. Any compromises or attempts to “balance” between human benefit and the emancipation of animals only reinforce their status as slaves. This is a rethinking of the relationship between humans and animals, in which they are no longer seen as means to human ends.</p>
