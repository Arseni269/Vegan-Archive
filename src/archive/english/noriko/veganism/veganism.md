<p>From this perspective, there is no reason to justify any form of animal use.</p>
<p>This includes even cases often seen as “harmless,” such as wool from sanctuary sheep or naturally shed feathers.</p>
<p>Once veganism is understood as a principle, the question is not how to divide acceptable use, but to stop treating animals as something for human use at all.</p>
<p>Even when situations are difficult, this does not create permission or justification.</p>
