<p>The idea of "Veganuary" reinforces the misconception that veganism is a diet that people can just try and ditch at their convenience.</p>

<p>This weakens a whole movement intended to elevate the moral status of non-human sentient individuals so that they're finally free from the human notion that they are on this planet at humans' disposal.</p>

<p>The fact that their only movement, based on the principle against all animal use and exploitation for human ends, is trivialized and reduced to a fleeting diet by the very same people who claim to hold this stance is a disservice to the animals.</p>
